## Reports and removal

Use [the asset report form](https://github.com/SamPatt/pelicans-art/issues/new?template=asset_report.yml) with the asset's Pouch URL, a short reason, and the action requested. Reports may concern spam, unsafe content, privacy violations, or material shared without permission. For rights claims, provide a public original-source link when available; the maintainer may request evidence privately before deciding.

Issues are public. Do not include private information, credentials, or sensitive evidence. Contact the repository owner privately when a report cannot safely be public; report security vulnerabilities according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). There is no guaranteed response time. Uploads are not reviewed before appearing, and the maintainer may remove assets following review. This reporting process does not expand the rights granted by any asset's license.
