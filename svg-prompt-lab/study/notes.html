SVG prompting: what is worth changing?

Read this after judging the images. These are a small screening study and the author's unblinded observations. Human preference scores have not yet been collected.

Six new briefs cover characters, props and backgrounds. Every brief includes the current agent guide and older browser prompt, alongside minimal instructions, explicit art direction, a short layout plan and a same-category SVG example. Each current-agent draft also has an independent repeat and one revision made after viewing its PNG.

Technical results

Compatibility checks are separate from aesthetic preference. A pass is not proof of good anatomy, complete brief fidelity or working animation.

What the research suggests testing

Planning objects, component shapes and layout, supplying SVG examples, and rendering a draft before correcting it are adaptations of Chat2SVG. Its full system also uses optimization and diffusion; this study does not reproduce its benchmark results.

IntroSVG and Render-in-the-Loop motivate testing rendered feedback. They use specialized trained systems, so a generic agent's correction pass should be tested rather than assumed effective.

The art-direction recipe draws on a practitioner's SVG skill account and PolyGlyph's prompting guide: deliberate style, composition and small-size legibility. These are candidate practices, not controlled evidence of superiority.

The abstract of Is Feedback All You Need? motivates comparing feedback with generating alternatives under a fixed candidate budget. Its full text was unavailable during this review; no outcome from that paper is asserted.

Your assessment

  1. Choose favorites within each brief before revealing recipes. Compare at normal and thumbnail size.
  2. Optionally rate fidelity, recognition, appeal, distinctiveness and production usability separately: 1 = unusable, 3 = usable with edits, 5 = strong as-is. Leave an axis unrated if you cannot assess it.
  3. Reveal methods and compare each recipe against the current agent control, retaining the browser comparison.
  4. Compare selecting from original + revision with selecting from original + fresh repeat. Count ties and revisions that made things worse. Two candidates are matched; tokens and elapsed inference time are not.
  5. Export ratings from the gallery before changing devices. They stay in local browser storage until exported.

Before changing the default

Confirm a promising recipe on held-out subjects with independent repeats, keeping the current control. Check shortlisted characters in the actual player for talking, gaze, emotion, facing and held props. The gallery's mouth/gaze probes are approximations. Test backgrounds behind actors at mobile size.

The controls share this study's technical contract and dimensions. They are harmonized first-pass controls, not exact end-to-end reproductions of every normal agent or browser step. Fresh agent contexts shared a filesystem with instruction-based file restrictions. The dispatch seed is not an inference seed. No generation token measurements or synthetic human ratings are provided.
