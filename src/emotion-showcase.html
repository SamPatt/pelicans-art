Key Techniques

Eyes:

  • Size: Bigger = surprised/excited, Smaller = angry/tired
  • Shape: Round = alert, Curved/closed = happy/smug
  • Position: Higher = alert, Lower = sad/tired
  • Pupil position: Can show where looking, or dilated for excitement
  • Highlights: Add white circles for "sparkle" in excited eyes

Eyebrows:

  • Angle up in center (∩) = sad/worried
  • Angle down INTO center (outer high, inner low) = angry
  • Raised high = surprised
  • One raised = skeptical/smug
  • Flat = neutral/tired

Asymmetry: Different left/right creates complex emotions (skeptical, confused, worried)
