Before the curtain goes up

Your agent can ask these together, and use sensible defaults for anything you leave open:

  1. What’s the premise? Bring an idea, or ask for two absurd suggestions.
  2. Portrait or landscape? Portrait for phone feeds; landscape for a wider stage.
  3. How long, and what kind of comedy? For example, 30–60 seconds of dry absurdity. Mention any audience or content boundaries.
  4. Who should sound like what? Share voice IDs from this sampler, describe the roles, or let the agent audition a couple for you.

Review the short plot and dialogue before the full render. For voices, a brief audition of each character’s actual line is more useful than guessing from a preset name.
